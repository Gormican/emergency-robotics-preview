The challenge

Rising costs.
A reason to
rethink care.

Healthcare costs keep rising. Finding a better way to deliver care starts with asking what could change.

At Emergency Robotics, we research the potential of AI and robotics in emergency medicine. Our ambition is straightforward: care that costs less, moves faster and serves people better.

That means looking beyond the technology to the people who would receive it and the care teams who would use it.

Less expensive

Investigate how technology could help reduce the cost of delivering care.

Faster

Explore how AI and robotics could help care teams work with fewer delays.

Better

Exploring how AI could bring medicine’s collective knowledge to the point of care.

The outcomes we are working toward, through research.
